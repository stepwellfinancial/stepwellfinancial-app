Keep a copy somewhere you control. Backup files land in places like your Downloads folder and cloud drives. An encrypted backup protects the file if someone else gets it. A plain backup does not. If you restore from a backup, it replaces what is in the app after you confirm. A restore that fails leaves your existing data as it was.

Passphrase. If you turn on the optional lock, you choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters. If you forget it, we can't reset it, and nobody can recover the locked data unless you have a backup you can open. Anyone who has your passphrase can read your data, so don't share it.

6. Accuracy: your inputs, your imports, and example figures

Every result in the app comes from the numbers you enter or import. If those are wrong, incomplete or out of date, the results will be too. Please check them.

Imports. You can import CSV, OFX and QFX files. The app reads them on your device. It limits the size of files it will read. It shows a preview before it saves anything, with a way to undo the whole import. If a date or amount can't be read, it shows the line as an error and doesn't guess. Different banks and programs write these files differently, so an import can still miss lines or read them in an unexpected way. Check the preview against your own records.

Calculations. Where the app works something out, such as a total, a difference or an illustration, it shows its inputs and describes its method. We work to make the arithmetic correct, but we can't promise the app is free of errors.

Examples and sample data. Figures marked "Sample data", "Example inputs", "example" or "illustration" show how a tool works. They are not about you and they are not a prediction.
